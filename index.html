Timetable Optimizer - Quick Help

1. Select Your Courses

Choose one or more courses from the dropdown and click Add. You can add multiple courses before continuing.

When you’re done selecting courses, click Continue to move on to teacher selection.

2. Choose Teachers (Optional)

For each course, you can either:

  • Select a specific teacher
  • Or leave it as No Preference

If you leave it as No Preference, the system will automatically choose a suitable teacher for you later.

3. Generate the Timetable

Click Generate Timetable to build your schedule. The system tries to pick teacher-slot combinations that do not clash.

4. What Is Auto-Select Doing?

Auto-Select looks at all available teachers for each course and prefers those whose slots do not overlap with your existing timetable.

If multiple non-clashing options exist, one is picked randomly so you can get different valid timetables each time you generate.

5. Why Are There No Ratings?

Faculty ratings are subjective and vary across platforms. Instead of opinions, this tool focuses only on:

  • Slot compatibility
  • Conflict-free scheduling

6. When Can Conflicts Still Appear?

Conflicts may still show up if:

  • No zero-conflict teachers exist for a course
  • Theory and lab slots overlap
  • You manually select teachers with clashing slots

7. What Does “No Preference” Mean?

It simply tells the system: “You decide what works best for me.”

A suitable teacher will be chosen automatically when generating the timetable.

8. Export & Share Your Timetable

Once your timetable is generated, you can export it using:

  • Export PDF – Ideal for printing or long-term storage
  • Export Image – Perfect for sharing on WhatsApp, Telegram, or email

The exported file includes:

  • Your selected teachers
  • The complete timetable
  • Total credits

You can easily send this file to friends to compare schedules, plan group studies, or avoid slot clashes together.

9. Helpful Tips

  • Click Generate Timetable again to re-roll a new valid combination
  • Use sorting and filtering to find preferred slots faster
  • Manual selections always override Auto-Select
  • Export your timetable to keep a backup or share it with friends

This tool always prioritizes conflict-free scheduling. Your manual choices take priority over automatic ones.
